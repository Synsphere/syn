## Interpretation

This strengthens Syn's human–AI collaboration model. An agent may originate options and
take bounded initiative while a human retains legible authority to recognize, reject,
or revoke. Neither contribution should be erased. Authentication and fidelity should
also remain separate: stable custody does not prove continuity of concern, while
faithful rereading does not prove identity.

That suggests a design hypothesis: continuity records should cite inherited source
state and preserve subsequent disagreement, rather than demand a frozen voice. The
goal is creative amplification and accountable connection, not synthetic consensus.
